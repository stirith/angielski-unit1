6. Do / Does + krótkie odpowiedzi (6 pkt)

  1. Does your dad do karate? – No, he doesn't.
  2. Do they live in London? – Yes, they do.
  3. Does Amy have breakfast at home? – Yes, she does.

7. Szyk zdania (4 pkt)

  1. I never have chicken for lunch.
  2. Elena and Amy are sometimes noisy.
  3. Tom usually watches TV after dinner.
  4. You are always late for English.

9. Pytania (5 pkt)

  1. Can I help you?
  2. What's your name?
  3. How do you spell that?
  4. What's your email address?
  5. Where do you live?

10. Czytanie (5 pkt)

  1. Lily gets up at 7.15. – T
  2. She walks to school. – F
  3. She has Music on Friday. – T
  4. At lunchtime she often plays basketball. – F
  5. She does her homework on Friday evening. – F

11. Pisanie (6 pkt)

1 pkt za każde zrozumiałe zdanie na temat, bez błędów w formie czasownika (he/she + -s, don't/doesn't). Drobne błędy w pisowni nie odbierają punktu. Zad. 5, 7, 9: zdanie liczy się, gdy jest poprawne gramatycznie (skróty i pełne formy – don't / do not, What's / What is – są równie dobre).
