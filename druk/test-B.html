10.Przeczytaj tekst. Zaznacz T (true – prawda) albo F (false – fałsz).   / 5 pkt

A day at my school

My name is Mia. I go to a boarding school in England, so I live at my school. I wake up at 6.45 and I have breakfast in the canteen. Lessons start at 9.00 and finish at 3.30. I love Art – I paint pictures every day! In the afternoon I sometimes go to the library. In the evening I do karate in the gym. I miss my parents, but I phone them on Sundays. My favourite day is Wednesday because we have two Art lessons.
zdanieTF
1. Mia lives at her school.
2. She has breakfast at home.
3. Lessons finish at 3.30.
4. She does karate in the library.
5. Her favourite day is Wednesday.

  5. Her favourite day is Wednesday. – T

11. Pisanie (6 pkt)

1 pkt za każde zrozumiałe zdanie na temat, bez błędów w formie czasownika (he/she + -s, don't/doesn't). Drobne błędy w pisowni nie odbierają punktu. Zad. 5, 7, 9: zdanie liczy się, gdy jest poprawne gramatycznie (skróty i pełne formy – don't / do not, What's / What is – są równie dobre).
