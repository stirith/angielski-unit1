10.Przeczytaj tekst. Zaznacz T (true – prawda) albo F (false – fałsz).   / 5 pkt

My school day

Hi, I'm Ben and I'm twelve. On weekdays I get up at 7 o'clock. I have toast and milk for breakfast and then I walk to school with my brother. Lessons start at 8.30. My favourite subject is P.E. because I love sport. At lunchtime I eat in the canteen with my friends. After school I play football in the park. I never watch TV in the evening – I read books or play the guitar. My favourite day is Saturday because I don't go to school!

  2. Anna doesn't do karate.

  4. My dad doesn't watch football.

6. Do / Does + krótkie odpowiedzi (6 pkt)

  1. Do you like Science? – Yes, I do.
  2. Does Lucas lose things? – Yes, he does.
  3. Do your friends play basketball? – No, they don't.

7. Szyk zdania (4 pkt)

  1. I always get up at 7 o'clock.
  2. She is never late for school.
  3. We sometimes have pizza on Friday.
  4. Tom often goes to the park.

9. Pytania (5 pkt)

  1. What's your name?
  2. How do you spell that?
  3. Where do you live?
  4. What's your email address?
  5. What's your phone number?

10. Czytanie (5 pkt)

  1. Ben gets up at 7 o'clock on weekdays. – T
  2. He goes to school by bus. – F
  3. His favourite subject is Maths. – F
  4. He has lunch in the canteen. – T
  5. He often watches TV in the evening. – F

11. Pisanie (6 pkt)

1 pkt za każde zrozumiałe zdanie na temat, bez błędów w formie czasownika (he/she + -s, don't/doesn't). Drobne błędy w pisowni nie odbierają punktu. Zad. 5, 7, 9: zdanie liczy się, gdy jest poprawne gramatycznie (skróty i pełne formy – don't / do not, What's / What is – są równie dobre).
